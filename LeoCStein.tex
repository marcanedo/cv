\section{Research Interests}
My research lie in the intersection of birational geometry and deformation theory of algebraic projective surfaces over the complex numbers. I have worked on the construction of $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein smoothings of singular algebraic projective surfaces, with particular interest in degenerations that preserve the Betti numbers and their applications into problems of algebraic geometry.
